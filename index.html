M17 hostfiles cache

Host files for M17 software, built every few hours from the DVRef API. They list M17 reflectors, M17-enabled URFD reflectors, and Vexillium (VEX) M17 instances.

  • M17Hosts.txt — DESIGNATOR address port per line
  • M17Hosts.json — full records

Reflector data provided by DVRef — https://dvref.com/, licensed CC BY 4.0; combined and reformatted.

Source: github.com/M17-Project/hostfiles
